# 📊 Statistical and Probability Analysis of Balaji Fast Food Sales Using R

## 📌 Project Overview
This project performs a comprehensive statistical and probability analysis on the Balaji Fast Food Sales dataset using R programming. The analysis focuses on understanding customer purchasing behavior, sales patterns, transaction distributions, and probability modeling using descriptive statistics, visualization techniques, correlation analysis, and Normal distribution concepts.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Clean and prepare transactional sales data
- Perform descriptive statistical analysis
- Analyze customer payment and purchasing behavior
- Visualize sales distributions and relationships
- Examine correlations between revenue drivers
- Apply probability theory using Normal distribution modeling

---

## 📂 Dataset Information
The dataset contains transactional fast-food sales records including:
- Invoice ID
- Sales Date
- Product Category
- Price per Unit
- Units Sold
- Sales Value
- Payment Type
- Sales Time

### Dataset Size
- **1,000 transactions**
- Multiple categorical and numerical variables

## 📊 Key Insights

✅ Fastfood contributes approximately 68.6% of total transactions  
✅ Cash payments are slightly more common than online payments  
✅ Customer activity remains relatively consistent throughout the day  
✅ Revenue is more strongly influenced by quantity sold than price changes  
✅ Most sales transactions fall within the moderate sales range  
✅ High-value transactions above 500 are relatively uncommon  

---

## 📈 Statistical Findings

### Correlation Analysis
- Units Sold vs Sales Value:
  - **Strong positive correlation (r = 0.7318)**
- Price per Unit vs Sales Value:
  - **Moderate positive correlation (r = 0.6372)**

### Probability Analysis
- Mean Sales Value:
  - **275.20**
- Probability of Sales > 500:
  - **13.57%**
- Probability of Sales between 200–600:
  - **58.75%**

---

## 🧠 Business Insights
- Sales growth is more dependent on increasing transaction volume than price increases
- Fastfood items dominate customer demand
- Moderate-value transactions represent the majority of sales activity
